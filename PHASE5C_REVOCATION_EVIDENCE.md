The [machine statistics](artifacts/myabe08/analysis/phase5c/20260926T134453Z-97db85981604/summary/EXP6_STATISTICS.csv), [state snapshots](artifacts/myabe08/benchmarks/phase5c/Exp6/SGX_DEBUG_GRAMINE_V1/20260926T094728Z-ba7e4c08e76c/state_memory.json) and [registration snapshots](artifacts/myabe08/benchmarks/phase5c/Exp6/SGX_DEBUG_GRAMINE_V1/20260926T094728Z-ba7e4c08e76c/registration_memory.json) are the sources for this table. These observations do not prove an asymptotic cost bound.

Across 120 repeated revoked lookup samples, enclave instrumentation reported zero user tokens, zero T0 pairings and zero caps emitted before rejection. The public lifecycle test confirmed an unconsumed, unexpired Auth issued before revocation remained usable for its original request, while new authorization after the revoke commit was blocked. Re-activation restored authorization. CT serialization, token representation and RK remained unchanged. These are TEST-time semantics under the single logical coordinator, not a production trusted-time or current-head guarantee.

The [adversarial record](artifacts/myabe08/benchmarks/phase5c/Exp6/SGX_DEBUG_GRAMINE_V1/20260926T094728Z-ba7e4c08e76c/adversarial.json) confirms exact replay idempotence and fail-closed rejection of stale, future, out-of-order and wrong-predecessor updates. Two conflicting updates prepared against the same predecessor were serialized: the first committed and the second was rejected. TEST-only injected freeze, head-ahead/lost-update and local rollback conditions caused `STATE_UNAVAILABLE`. This does not establish hardware anti-rollback or distributed linearizability.

At RL=1000, separate 30-second load windows recorded target and achieved rates for active and revoked classes. Active windows completed 4/8/11/15 requests across the four target fractions; revoked windows completed 16/32/47/48. At the 100% revoked target, offered rate was 2.081 requests/s and observed achieved rate 1.595 requests/s, with median/p95 latency 508.688/566.997 ms and queue-delay median/p95 3378.261/6547.163 ms. See the [full load table](artifacts/myabe08/analysis/phase5c/20260926T134453Z-97db85981604/tables/TABLE_EXP6_REQUEST_LOAD.md); these fixed windows do not establish production capacity.

The Exp7 [freshness observations](artifacts/myabe08/benchmarks/phase5c/Exp7/SGX_DEBUG_GRAMINE_V1/20260926T133402Z-d874ac0fa211/freshness.json) found 50 unique values and zero observed collisions for each of request nonce, K1q, K_tau,q, T0q and cap across 50 requests. The [equality record](artifacts/myabe08/benchmarks/phase5c/Exp7/SGX_DEBUG_GRAMINE_V1/20260926T133402Z-d874ac0fa211/equality.json) shows 100/100 same-context token equality, 0/100 direct cross-context equality, and 50/50 same-context AF successes. The [known leakage record](artifacts/myabe08/benchmarks/phase5c/Exp7/SGX_DEBUG_GRAMINE_V1/20260926T133402Z-d874ac0fa211/known_leakage.json) shows W stable in 50/50 same-user/same-CT comparisons. Different CT and different user were each compared once and were unequal; those single observations support no general independence claim. The controlled canary/log scans passed, but DataID, context, same-context token equality, AF hits, J/I, timing/count, ciphertext identity, revocation behavior and stable W remain observable.

All these observations belong to `SGX_DEBUG_GRAMINE_V1` with `QVL_VERIFIED_OUTDATED_TCB_DEBUG_FEASIBILITY_V1`, QVL `SGX_QL_QV_RESULT_OUT_OF_DATE`, strict RA2 `BLOCKED_BY_TCB_POLICY`, and `TEST_SINGLE_LOGICAL_COORDINATOR`. There is no production persistent SGX state, hardware anti-rollback, trusted time, production remote current-head or release enclave; side channels remain unaddressed and the formal security proof remains open. No production revocation, full anonymity, unlinkability proof or zero-cost claim follows from this dataset.
